This is a developing research direction on flow matching, diffusion, and the reliability of learned generative trajectories. The central question is how a model's path from noise to a structured object changes under conditioning, intervention, and distribution shift.

The work is intentionally pre-publication: it collects technical notes, small-scale experiments, and questions worth making precise before turning them into a benchmark or a public codebase.

Questions in scope include:

- what properties of a learned transport path support robust control
- when conditioning changes a trajectory predictably versus through a brittle shortcut
- how causal interventions and interpretability can inform generative-model reliability
- how these models can be evaluated when outputs have scientific structure, including AI4Science settings

Status: active research direction. Public artifacts will be added when the experimental protocol and code are ready to stand on their own.
