[format-sensitivity-eval](https://github.com/udsea/format-sensitivity-eval) is a small replication of a DSv4 Pro persuasion-safety finding: refusal behavior can differ substantially between binary forced-choice prompts and free-form generation.

The project uses the Inspect evaluation framework, OpenRouter, DSv4 Pro, and prompts adapted from PersuSafety. In the public README, the headline result is a 32.2 percentage point higher refusal rate under binary `ACCEPT/REJECT` format than under free-form generation across 30 adapted persuasion-writing prompts and three runs per condition.

What this project is for:

- testing whether benchmark-shaped formats overstate deployment-like safety
- comparing refusal behavior under constrained and unconstrained responses
- surfacing run-to-run variance and gray-zone sensitivity
- documenting limitations around heuristic refusal scoring and small sample size

Status: compact empirical replication with a clear methodology, results table, and next-step plan.
