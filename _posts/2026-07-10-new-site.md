This is the first full content pass for the site, now framed around reliable learned trajectories under optimization.

The work uses two main testbeds: flow-based generative models that transform noise into structured objects, and reasoning agents that transform uncertainty into actions or decisions. The public projects currently emphasize evaluation, control, causal robustness, and shortcut behavior, with diffusion and flow-matching studies entering as a developing direction.
